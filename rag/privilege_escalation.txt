TITLE: Privilege Escalation

CATEGORY: Access Control

DESCRIPTION:
Privilege escalation involves obtaining permissions beyond those normally assigned to an account.

POSSIBLE LOG PATTERNS:
- Attempts to access administrative resources
- Permission changes
- Unexpected administrative activity
- Access to restricted resources
- Changes to account privileges

INVESTIGATION:
Review account permissions, administrative actions, access logs, and the sequence of events before and after the suspicious activity.

DEFENSIVE GUIDANCE:
Use least-privilege access, monitor administrative activity, maintain audit logs, and review permission changes.
