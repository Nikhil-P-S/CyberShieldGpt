TITLE: Account Compromise

CATEGORY: Account Security

DESCRIPTION:
An account compromise occurs when an unauthorized person obtains or uses valid account credentials.

POSSIBLE INDICATORS:
- Unusual authentication activity
- Multiple failed attempts followed by successful login
- Unexpected access to sensitive resources
- Login activity inconsistent with normal behavior
- Unexpected changes to account settings

INVESTIGATION:
Review authentication history, source addresses, access activity, account changes, and other related events.

RECOMMENDED RESPONSE:
Verify the account owner's activity, investigate suspicious sessions, review credentials, and apply the organization's incident response procedures where appropriate.

NOTE:
An unusual login pattern alone does not prove that an account has been compromised.
